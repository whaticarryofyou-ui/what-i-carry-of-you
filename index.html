The project

Some things can't be said out loud. They can only be written.

We all carry something. A person we love, or one we lost. A fear we haven't said out loud. A hope we're still holding on to. A version of ourselves we're slowly learning to become. These letters are for all of it. And for you, if you've ever felt something deeply and never quite found the words.

A letter here isn't meant to be scrolled past. It asks for a quiet moment, and for your whole attention. You read it once, slowly. The ink fades. And if something in it stayed with you, you give it to the person who came to mind. That's how it travels: from one heart to another, one star at a time.

New letters arrive when they're ready, without a calendar or a plan. Each one is written when something inside asks to be said.

How a letter travels

Read it once. Then let it go.

  1. 1Break the seal

    Someone thought of you. Before you read, you'll find the words they chose for you.

  2. 2Read it slowly

    Give it a quiet moment. There's only one reading, and when you reach the end, the ink gently fades. The stars you lit remain.

  3. 3Light a star

    When a paragraph reaches you, light its star. The next person will see that it touched someone before them.

  4. 4Give it to someone

    Think of who should read it next. Leave a few words if you'd like, or nothing at all. Your star joins its sky, without your name.
